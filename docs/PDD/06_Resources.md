# 📚 06. Resources & Further Reading

For deep dives into specific topics, we recommend the following expert resources.

## 📘 Official Salesforce Documentation

- **[Modular Development with Unlocked Packages](https://developer.salesforce.com/blogs/2018/06/working-with-modular-development-and-unlocked-packages-part-4)**
  _Why read_: Deep dive into package strategies from Salesforce Developers Blog.

- **[Unlocked Package Creation Guide](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/unlocked-packages-for-customers/build-your-first-unlocked-package)**
  _Why read_: Step-by-step CLI instructions for creating your first package.

## 🧠 Expert Articles & Best Practices

- **[Breaking Your Org into Packages (Salesforce Ben)](https://www.salesforceben.com/breaking-your-org-into-packages-salesforce-dx/)**
  _Why read_: A practical roadmap for moving from "Happy Soup" to modular packages.

- **[Unlocked Packages: A Comprehensive Guide](https://www.salesforceben.com/unlocked-packages-in-salesforce-a-comprehensive-guide-for-developers/)**
  _Why read_: Explains the different types of packages and when to use them.

- **[Architect Adaptable Solutions with a Packaging Mindset](https://medium.com/salesforce-architects/architect-adaptable-solutions-with-a-packaging-mindset-af9a1317ec43)**
  _Why read_: High-level architectural perspective on modular design from Salesforce Architects.

## 🗣 Community Discussions

- **[Reddit: Strategies for moving from Org Based to Package Based Development](https://www.reddit.com/r/salesforce/comments/1csvo9o)**
